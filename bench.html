How the labels work

Measured: an observed counter, clock or sensor, or a rate calculated from those observations. Estimated: sampled rates from batched backend progress, or a conservative capacity allowance. Unavailable: the backend or host exposes no suitable observation. Historical: a saved run, not a current rate; original measurement status remains attached.

Restic packed bytes describe added compressed data, not on-wire traffic. Filesystem sync returning does not certify device durability. Cache state is uncontrolled, and a local fixture is not an iPad transport test.
